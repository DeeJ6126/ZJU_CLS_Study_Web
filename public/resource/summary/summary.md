Restoration ecology is a branch of modern applied ecology that rapidly developed since the 1980s. The key to ecological restoration is the restoration of system functions and the construction of a reasonable structure. This course mainly introduces the emergence, development, theory, and content system of restoration ecology; Ecological restoration engineering and technical principles; Ecological restoration techniques for farmland and mining soil; Ecological restoration of lake water bodies; Restoration and construction of degraded forests and ecological public welfare forests; Grassland ecological restoration technology; Restoration and reconstruction techniques for small watershed ecosystems; Ecological restoration and reconstruction techniques for ecologically fragile zones; Ecological protection technology in nature reserves.
